1Summary

Spin Wheel does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. The app works completely offline.

2Information stored on your device

To make the app convenient, Spin Wheel saves the following only on your own device:

  • the list of options you type into the wheel;
  • your app settings, such as sound on or off and the “Remove winner after spin” switch.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by using the Reset button, clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s settings, or uninstalling the app.

3Permissions

Spin Wheel does not request permission to access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, phone number or the internet.

4Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no user accounts. It does not use any online service.

5Children

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

6Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
